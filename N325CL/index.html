    Know the difference

    SR22 or SR22TN?

    Same airframe, same proven IO-550-N engine. They look alike on the ramp and fly very differently once the air gets thin.

    SR22
    Normally aspirated
    • Continental IO-550-N · 310 hp
    • Power fades as you climb: roughly 3% per 1,000 ft (rule of thumb)
    • Maximum operating altitude 17,500 ft

    Tornado Alley turbonormalizing

    Sea-level power to 25,000 ft

    A normally aspirated engine loses power as the air thins. Turbonormalizing feeds the IO-550-N sea-level manifold pressure all the way up, with dual turbos, dual intercoolers and GAMIjectors, and without over-boosting the engine.

    Normally aspirated curve approximated from the standard atmosphere. 25,000 ft is the SR22 Turbo’s certified ceiling.

    New MT 4-blade composite · 25+ hrs

    The four-blade advantage

    Scimitar airfoil for improved thrust on take-off and climb, turbine-smooth with markedly reduced vibration, added ground clearance, and nickel-cobalt leading edges on a field-repairable, rebuildable hub. STC SA02924CH.
